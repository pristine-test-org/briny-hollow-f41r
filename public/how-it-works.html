How the list works

Every item you add lands on one shared list, and everyone on your team sees it the moment it is saved.

  1. Add an item from the landing page.
  2. Mark it done when the work is finished.
  3. Finished items stay visible with a check mark.

Items are kept for as long as your account is active, and only signed-in people can read them.
